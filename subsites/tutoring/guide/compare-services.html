Peer tutoring is where students work with other CSP students who have taken the course at CSP, or an equivalent course at a different institution, and have a strong command of the course material.

Pick this service if you:

  • Want help from another CSP student.
  • Would like to meet with a tutor in-person or over Zoom.
  • Would like a tutor who has taken the course you need help with or an equivalent course before.

Brainfuse tutoring is a 3rd party tutoring resource where students can live-chat professional tutors with their questions. For some subjects, students can seek help 24/7.

Pick this service if you:

  • Need help immediately in a course or with understanding an assignment.
  • Would like to converse with a tutor by typing out your questions.
  • Can't find the course you are looking for or if a peer tutor isn't available when you need help.
